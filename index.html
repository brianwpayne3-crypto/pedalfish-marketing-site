Why PedalFish

Software should strengthen an independent shop, not standardize it.

PedalFish is being shaped inside actual bicycle-service work by mechanics and owner-operators. The goal isn't to automate the shop or prescribe one way to run it.

It starts with service because that's where the work gets messy: customer expectations, diagnosis, authorization, parts, waiting, repair work and the record of what happened.
