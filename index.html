Being great at fixing bikes shouldn't require being great at every operational task it takes to run a bike shop.

Built to understand the shop

Not another chatbot.

The intelligence in PedalFish is being built on the actual operational record of the shop — customers, bikes, repairs, parts, commitments and work in progress.

The more of the operation PedalFish understands, the more useful it can become: helping surface what needs attention, which jobs are stuck, what needs to be ordered, who needs an update and where the shop is losing time.

It starts with service

Build intelligence on top of the work that's actually happening.

Service is the first deep PedalFish workflow because it creates a durable operational record from the moment a bike comes in until it goes home.

Why PedalFish

Software should strengthen an independent shop, not standardize it.

PedalFish is being shaped inside actual bicycle-shop work by mechanics and owner-operators. The goal isn't to automate the shop or prescribe one way to run it.

Service is the foundation, not the boundary. The same operational understanding can extend into customer communication, parts and procurement, shop coordination and the decisions an owner has to make every day.

Early users

Run a bike shop? We'd like to hear how you work.

PedalFish is being built alongside real shop operations. We're interested in talking with independent shops, mechanics and mobile repair operators who want to help shape what comes next.
